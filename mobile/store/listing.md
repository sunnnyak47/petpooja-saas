## Full description
```
MSRM Owner puts your whole restaurant in your pocket — point of sale, kitchen
display, online orders, inventory and live reports in one app, built to keep
working even when the internet doesn't.

• POINT OF SALE — take dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders, split bills, apply
  discounts and settle by cash or card in seconds.
• KITCHEN — fire orders straight to the kitchen display, track prep times and
  auto-86 items when stock runs out.
• ONLINE & QR ORDERING — take orders from your own QR menu with no commission.
• INVENTORY — recipe-based stock deduction, low-stock alerts and purchase orders.
• LIVE REPORTS — sales, best-sellers, per-channel margins and one-tap end-of-day.
• WORKS OFFLINE — keep selling with no connection; everything syncs automatically
  when you're back online.
• MULTI-OUTLET — manage every venue from one login.

Built for Australian and Indian restaurants — GST-ready, with the payment and
delivery integrations that matter in your market.
```

## Age rating / content
No objectionable content → 4+ (iOS) / Everyone (Android). Business tool; no ads,
no UGC feeds. Declare: does NOT collect precise location for tracking; uses camera
(QR/barcode scan) and photos (menu images) with on-first-use prompts.

## Data-safety / privacy declarations (Play Data Safety + Apple Privacy)
Collected: account info (name, email, phone), business/transaction data. Used for
app functionality only; not sold; not used for cross-app tracking. Encrypted in
transit. Users can request deletion. (Mirror the wording in the privacy policy.)
